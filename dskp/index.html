Curriculum and assessment

DSKP

The Dokumen Standard Kurikulum dan Pentaksiran sets out what pupils should learn and how teachers can assess progress. This page suggests how to use the quest with KSSR English Year 4 in a national primary school (Sekolah Kebangsaan).

Learning standards in practice

  • 2.1.5 · Speaking

    Describe people and objects. After a clue, ask pupils to say where Pip or a landmark is in a complete sentence.

  • 3.2.2 · Reading

    Locate specific details in a short text. Give pupils a one- or two-paragraph mission briefing and ask them to underline the details that tell them where to look.

  • 4.2.4 · Writing

    Write descriptions of people and objects. Have pupils label a scene and write their own clues, such as “Pip is between the two towers.”

Check for understanding

  1. Model one clue by pointing out the preposition and the reference object.
  2. Ask a partner to solve two pupil-written clues using the scene.
  3. Compare the selected location with what the pupil said or wrote.

Use game feedback to spot words pupils need to practise. Use their spoken and written work to make classroom assessment judgments.
